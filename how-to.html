FOR HOSTS

Create & edit a Moment

  1. Choose the create action in Stelli and enter your Moment’s name, date, and location.
  2. Add an optional Custom Image for the cover, then save.
  3. Use the Host dashboard to record, invite, and manage playback. Open Actions → Edit Moment to update its details.

The cover is part of the Guest experience and is available to review in Edit Moment. It is separate from the Photo Gallery.

When you’re finished hosting, choose Actions → End Moment. Closing the dashboard simply takes you back; it does not end the Moment.

Invite people & join a Moment

As a Host

Tap Invite to share an invitation, or use Actions → Copy Moment Code. Send the link or six-character code to the people you want to join.

As a Guest

Sign in to Stelli, then open the Host’s invite link or enter their code using the join action. Open the joined Moment to get ready to listen.

Use the full invite link shared by the Host. If an older link no longer works, ask for a new invitation or enter the current code. To leave, use the Guest’s Leave action. You can rejoin an available Moment unless the Host has removed your access.

Record your own audio

  1. On the Host dashboard, tap Record.
  2. Allow microphone access when asked, then start your recording.
  3. Stop when you’re ready. You can give the recording a name before saving it.
  4. Wait for uploading to finish before cueing the clip for Guests.

Record a short test first to check your level and surroundings. Music, ambience, cheers, and spoken recordings can all be part of a Moment; only share audio you have permission to use.

Build your Clip Library

Your Clip Library keeps your audio ready to use in Moments. Choose a saved clip when adding audio to a Moment; removing its playlist entry does not delete the saved Library asset.

Bring an audio file

Importing is optional. Use the import action in Clip Library and choose an M4A, MP3, WAV, or AAC file from Files. Imports must be 100 MB or smaller, and duration limits depend on your plan. Wait for validation and upload to finish.

Adding a clip to a Moment makes it available in that Moment. Your private Library is not a shared folder for Guests.

Cue a clip & listen together

For the best synchronized playback, use a stable Wi‑Fi or cellular connection and keep Stelli open. Clips can also play in sync while Stelli is in the background or your device is locked, provided you remain joined to the Moment, the clip finishes preloading, and your device receives the playback cue in time. Network conditions and device background restrictions can prevent playback. If a device isn’t ready before the scheduled start, Stelli skips the clip on that device rather than playing it late.

Stelli preloads upcoming clips using your internet connection. Data use depends on clip size and whether a download is needed; cellular charges may apply.

The Host controls playback in a Host-led Moment. Use the clip and playlist controls to prepare the next sound, start it, pause, or stop. Use Queue Next when you want to announce the next clip, or the playlist controls to play a sequence. A heads-up lets you notify Guests before a clip starts.

Give Guests time to join and get their audio ready before starting. If Stelli says some Guests aren’t ready, wait for them or use the offered start-anyway option knowing they may miss the synchronized start.

As a Guest, open the joined Moment, check your volume, and follow the Host’s cue. Headphones are useful when you want to listen personally without adding sound to the room.

Share photos & choose a cover

Custom Image / cover

Choose or change this in Edit Moment. Let the image finish saving before leaving. The cover and your profile avatar are two different images.

Photo Gallery

Hosts add Moment photos from the gallery controls. Guests can use Add Photos when the Host has enabled contributions.

Guest contributions wait for Host approval before appearing in the shared gallery. Hosts can approve or decline pending photos; Guests can withdraw their own pending submissions and remove their own shared photos.

Explore Discover & follow Hosts

Discover is a different way to listen: open an available nearby Moment and explore its recorded audio at your own pace. Sign in and allow location access so Stelli can show what is available within the Moment’s listening area.

Hosts can configure a Moment for Discover with a location, listening radius, and recorded content. Review the Discover settings before publishing; an ordinary private invitation does not automatically publish a Moment to Discover.

Follow Hosts you want to hear from again. In Settings, you can choose whether to be notified when followed users start new Moments and when you gain a follower.

Safety & Guest access

Open a Moment’s safety controls to report content or block its Host. For a report, choose a reason, add useful details, and submit. Blocking a Host and reporting content are separate actions.

Review or unblock Hosts in Settings → Safety → Blocked Hosts. Hosts can manage Guest access from the People controls, including removing a Guest when needed.

For help, choose Contact Stelli Support in Settings or email support@stelliapp.com. Please describe what happened without including passwords or verification codes. See our Community Guidelines for what belongs on Stelli.

Stelli Free & Host Plus

Playback conditions apply to both Free and Host Plus. A subscription expands Host limits; it does not guarantee synchronized or background playback. Read the playback conditions before subscribing.

You can start on Stelli Free. Host Plus expands the Host’s limits; Guests don’t need the Host’s subscription to join an invitation.

Make Stelli yours

In Settings, update your profile name, avatar, and bio; manage notification preferences; choose playback behavior when your device is muted; and turn sound effects on or off.

Settings also contains support, blocked Hosts, Host Plus, and links to the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Account deletion is available there too; follow the confirmation steps and keep the deletion receipt if you need to check its progress.

Quick answers

My invite link or code doesn’t work.

Check that you’re signed in and using the full, current invite or all six characters of the code. Ask the Host whether the Moment is still available. If you blocked the Host, review Blocked Hosts in Settings. A Host removal can also prevent rejoining.

Can clips play while Stelli is backgrounded or my screen is locked?

For the best synchronized playback, use a stable Wi‑Fi or cellular connection and keep Stelli open. Clips can also play in sync while Stelli is in the background or your device is locked, provided you remain joined to the Moment, the clip finishes preloading, and your device receives the playback cue in time. Network conditions and device background restrictions can prevent playback. If a device isn’t ready before the scheduled start, Stelli skips the clip on that device rather than playing it late.

I can’t hear the audio.

Check your volume, selected audio output, and internet connection. Reopen the joined Moment and wait for the Host’s next cue. Check the muted-device playback preference in Settings. If a recording is still uploading, let it finish first.

My photo or cover hasn’t appeared.

Wait for the upload to complete. A Guest photo also needs Host approval. For a Custom Image, open Edit Moment to check the saved cover; it is separate from the Photo Gallery. If saving fails, retry while connected.

Why was my import or recording stopped?

Check your plan’s duration limit. Imports also need a supported audio format and must be 100 MB or smaller. Use a shorter recording or file, or review Host Plus in Settings.

Can I use one account on two phones at once?

Stelli uses one active device session per account. For a Host-and-Guest setup, use a different Stelli account on each phone.
