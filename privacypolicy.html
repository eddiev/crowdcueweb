Privacy Policy

v1.6 · Last Updated: November 3, 2026

Welcome

Privacy matters because shared moments only work when people trust the experience.

Stelli is operated by Stellation LLC. Stelli helps people create and participate in shared audio Moments, including Host-led playback and self-paced listening through Discover. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when it may be shared, and the choices you have.

By using Stelli, you acknowledge that your information will be handled as described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use Stelli.

Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect information such as:

  • Email address
  • Sign in with Apple account information, when you choose that sign-in method
  • Unique account identifier
  • Display name
  • Profile photo
  • Optional profile details, such as a biography

Moment Information

When you create or participate in a Moment, we may collect information including:

  • Moment title
  • Date and time
  • Location, if you choose to provide one
  • Host and Guest participation
  • Moment settings and preferences

Following and Participation

We store the Hosts you follow, follower relationships, Moment membership, participation and playback state, and related preferences so we can provide following, notifications, shared playback, and moderation features.

Content You Share

Stelli is built around content shared by its community. Depending on how you use the app, you may create or upload audio recordings, import audio clips into your Clip Library, upload photos, profile images, event images, and other content you choose to contribute to a Moment.

Safety Reports and Blocking

If you report a Moment, we collect your account identifier, the Moment and Host identifiers, the reason you select, and the details you provide. Restricted support notifications help us investigate and respond to reports. We also store your blocked-Host list to hide those Hosts and prevent you from joining their Moments. Reports are not shared with the reported user.

Clip Library

When you choose to import or record an audio clip, we collect and store the audio file and related information, such as its filename, title, tags, duration, file size, and content type, in your Clip Library. Clips are associated with your account and are private unless you choose to add one to a Moment, where it may become available to eligible participants.

You retain ownership of the content you create. Our use of that content is described in our Terms of Service.

Purchases and Subscriptions

If you purchase Host Plus through Apple, we receive and store transaction identifiers, the purchased plan, subscription status and expiration information, and account-linked purchase information needed to verify and restore access, prevent fraud, and handle subscription changes. Apple processes your payment; Stelli does not receive your full payment-card details.

Website Updates and Support

If you sign up for launch updates on our website, we collect your email address, consent record, sign-up source, and sign-up time. We use these records to send the updates you requested and manage your subscription to those emails. Our sign-up service also uses a hashed network-address value to limit abusive requests. You can leave the list by emailing support@stelliapp.com. If you use our website contact form, we collect your name, email address, and message to respond to your request. We send your message to our support inbox using our email provider. The form also uses a hashed network-address value to limit abusive requests. If you contact support by email, we receive the information and attachments you choose to send.

Device and Technical Information

To help operate and improve Stelli, we may collect device identifiers, push notification tokens, app version, device type, crash reports, diagnostic information, network and playback performance information, and app usage information. Service providers may also process network addresses and derive general geographic information to operate, secure, and measure the service.

Permissions We Request

Stelli only requests permissions when they are needed for features you choose to use.

Microphone

Used when you choose to record audio clips to save in your Clip Library or share in a Moment.

Camera

Used to scan QR codes for joining or sharing Moments.

Photos

Used when you choose to upload profile photos, Moment images, or other pictures. When available, Stelli uses Apple’s photo picker so you can select specific photos without granting access to your entire photo library. Selected photos are uploaded when you add them. We may compare uploaded files to avoid keeping duplicate photos in the same Moment.

Location

Used when you choose location-based features, such as adding a location to a Moment or viewing eligible Moments in Discover. Depending on your permission settings, location information may be precise or approximate. When a Host saves a Moment location, we store the selected location and coordinates. When discovery is enabled, Stelli uses your location while you are using the app to determine whether a Moment is within the Host’s selected radius. Stelli does not use background location for this feature.

Notifications

Used to notify you about invitations, updates to Moments you are participating in, activity from Hosts you follow, and important account or service notifications. You can change these permissions at any time through your device settings.

How We Use Information

We use information to create and manage your account; authenticate users; let you host and join Moments; store and synchronize the content you choose to share; deliver notifications and requested website updates; verify and manage subscription access; improve Stelli’s performance and reliability; detect bugs and troubleshoot issues; help protect the security of our users and services; and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

How Information Is Shared

Because Stelli is designed around shared experiences, some information is visible to other people using the service. Depending on how a Moment works, other participants may be able to see your display name, profile photo, participation in a Moment, content you choose to contribute, and basic information about the Moment itself.

Profile information, such as your display name and profile image, may also be visible to other signed-in Stelli users. Following a Host creates an account-linked relationship; Hosts may see information about their followers.

When a Host publishes a Discover Moment, eligible signed-in listeners can find and listen to it without following the Host or receiving a private invitation. The Moment’s information, selected location, and content made available through Discover may be visible to those listeners. The app uses the Host’s selected location area to present Discover Moments; that area is not a guarantee of confidentiality or a security boundary. Do not publish content in Discover that you intend to keep limited to private invitees.

People who receive a valid Moment link or code may be able to join, subject to the Moment’s participation settings and moderation. Share invitations only with the people you intend to invite.

Clips in your Clip Library are not shared with other users unless you choose to add them to a Moment. New Guest photo submissions are visible only to the submitting Guest and Host until the Host approves publication in the shared gallery. Hosts may decline submissions and remove eligible photos from their Moment’s gallery; Guests can remove their own eligible gallery contributions where the feature is available.

We do not share your personal information with advertisers. We may share information with trusted service providers that help us operate Stelli, as described below.

Service Providers

We rely on trusted third-party providers to operate Stelli. These providers help us with user authentication, cloud data storage, file storage, push notifications, analytics, crash reporting, and security. These include Google/Firebase for cloud services, notifications, analytics, and diagnostics, Resend for transactional and support-notification email, and Apple for sign-in and App Store transactions. Providers handling personal information on our behalf must use it for the services they provide to us and protect it consistently with this Policy and applicable requirements, including protections no less protective than those required by Apple’s App Review Guidelines. Apple also handles Apple Account and payment information under its own privacy terms. See Firebase privacy information and Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Analytics and Diagnostics

We use analytics and diagnostic tools to better understand how Stelli is used and to improve the service. This information may include app interactions, device information, crash reports, and performance information. These records may be associated with device or installation identifiers and with account-related content or participation. They should not be understood to be anonymous. If you choose to send a diagnostic export to support, we use it to investigate the issue you report.

Data Retention

We keep information only as long as it is reasonably necessary to operate Stelli, provide the services you request, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the security and integrity of the service. Different types of information may be retained for different periods. Backup copies may remain for a limited time after information is deleted.

In-app safety report records are scheduled for removal after 90 days through a daily cleanup process. Support correspondence and records needed for an ongoing investigation, legal obligation, or dispute may be retained as reasonably necessary for those purposes.

Your Choices

You can update your profile information, manage following relationships, control device permissions and notification settings, rename or remove clips in your Clip Library, and remove eligible gallery photos. Removing a clip from a Moment removes it from that Moment’s playlist only. Removing a clip from Clip Library deletes it from your Library and may remove associated Moment placements. Removing a gallery photo removes it from the gallery; limited backup, legal, or security retention may still apply. Stelli cannot remove copies that other people have independently saved or shared outside the service.

Account Deletion

In Stelli, open Settings and select Delete Account to request deletion of your account and associated personal information. You will be asked to confirm and sign in again. You can check the saved deletion receipt on the same device from the Sign In screen. Account deletion includes your profile and uploaded content, including content shared in Moments, except information we are required or permitted to retain for limited legal, fraud-prevention, security, or transaction-record purposes. Deletion is processed in the background with retries; you can use the saved receipt to check completion. If a request has not completed within 24 hours, contact support. Minimal deletion and security records are kept for 30 days after completion and then scheduled for removal; the receipt is available during that period. Other limited legal or backup retention may apply as described above. Contact support@stelliapp.com for questions about your data or an existing deletion request.

Deleting an account, leaving a Moment, or uninstalling Stelli does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage or cancel Host Plus in your Apple Account to stop future renewals. Apple may retain transaction information under its own policies.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information under applicable privacy laws. These rights may include the ability to request access to the personal information we maintain about you, request that inaccurate information be corrected, request deletion of your personal information subject to certain legal or operational exceptions, and request additional information about how your information is collected, used, or shared.

To exercise any applicable privacy rights, please contact us using the information provided at the end of this Privacy Policy. We will respond to requests in accordance with applicable law.

Children’s Privacy

Stelli is not intended for children under 13 years of age. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.

International Users

Stelli is operated from the United States. If you access Stelli from outside the United States, you understand that your information may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate. Privacy and data protection laws in those countries may differ from those in your country of residence.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to help protect your information. However, no method of transmitting or storing information is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as Stelli evolves. When we make material changes, we will update the Last Updated date and may provide additional notice within the app.
